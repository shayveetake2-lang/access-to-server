Important Requirement: ZeroTier Network Connection Needed REQUIRED

This media server operates inside an encrypted, private ZeroTier virtual LAN. To connect and stream movies & TV shows from any device outside local Wi-Fi, you must install ZeroTier and have your node authorized by the admin.

1

Install & Connect to ZeroTier (Required)

Download and install the ZeroTier One app on your phone, laptop, or desktop. Join the server's 16-digit network and ask the administrator to approve your Node ID.

2

Create a Free Plex Account

Go to plex.tv/sign-up and create a free Plex account using your email. No subscription is needed — a free account is all you require to access this server.

3

Ask an Admin for an Invite

Contact the server admin via the ServerFlow dashboard or directly, and provide your Plex account email and your ZeroTier Node ID. The admin will authorize your ZeroTier connection and send you a library share invite from within Plex.

4

Accept the Invite & Open Plex

Check your email for the Plex share invitation and accept it. Ensure ZeroTier is connected, then click the Open Plex Web App button above (or direct IP: http://10.247.192.231:32400/web) to browse and stream movies!

5

Browse & Stream Anywhere

Navigate to Movies or TV Shows in Plex. You can also install the official Plex app on iOS, Android, Apple TV, or smart TVs and stream over ZeroTier.

Troubleshooting
  • › Server offline or unreachable in Plex? Verify that ZeroTier is connected on your device (status should say OK with a green dot). Without ZeroTier active, the server IP 10.247.192.231 cannot be reached outside home Wi-Fi.
  • › No library showing? Make sure you've accepted the admin's share invite via your Plex email before opening the app.
  • › Playback buffering? Switch the Plex streaming quality to a lower setting (720p or 1080p original) under Settings → Quality.
  • › Server not appearing? Confirm the server is online via the Dashboard. If it just went down, check back shortly or contact an admin.

Step-by-Step Guide: Connect & Stream Media

Follow these 5 simple steps to get connected and start enjoying your movies and music.

1

Download & Install ZeroTier

Choose your device platform from the download grid above and install the app. Launch ZeroTier One.

💡 Mobile Note: On iPhones or Android devices, system prompts will ask permission to add a VPN configuration. Tap Allow so ZeroTier can route traffic to the server.
2

Join the Server's ZeroTier Network

In the ZeroTier One app, select "Join New Network" (on mobile, tap the + button). Enter the 16-digit Network ID provided by the server administrator.

16-Digit ZeroTier Network ID
Ask the ServerFlow Admin for your unique Network ID
Ensure "Allow Managed IP" and "Allow Global" checkboxes are enabled when joining.
3

Get Authorized by the Admin

Because our server network is private and secured against unauthorized outsiders, all newly joined devices initially display ACCESS_DENIED.

Find your 10-digit Node ID / Address in the ZeroTier app (displayed at the bottom or top of your screen, e.g. a1b2c3d4e5). Send this 10-digit ID to the admin. Once the admin clicks "Authorize", your connection is live!

4

Confirm Connection Status

Within 10 seconds of admin approval, your ZeroTier status indicator turns OK (Green), and your device receives an IP address in the 10.247.192.x subnet. You are now securely bridged to the home server.

5

Launch Plex Media Server

You are ready to stream Plex movies and TV shows! Remember: ZeroTier is strictly required ONLY for Plex. Music from Aether Audio streams directly without ZeroTier.

❓ Why is ZeroTier required instead of regular port forwarding?

Residential internet providers frequently change IP addresses or use CGNAT (Carrier-Grade NAT) which prevents direct external connections. ZeroTier establishes an encrypted, peer-to-peer virtual tunnel directly into the server without opening risky ports to web crawlers and malicious bots on the public internet.

📱 How do I stream on my phone or tablet when away from home?

Whenever you leave your home Wi-Fi and want to watch Plex movies & TV, simply open the ZeroTier One app and flip the toggle switch to ON. Once the VPN key icon appears in your device status bar, open the Plex app.
Note: ZeroTier is NOT required at all for music — you can stream music directly from the Aether Audio web player anytime without ZeroTier.

⚡ Does ZeroTier slow down my general internet browsing?

No! ZeroTier operates in split-tunnel mode. Only packets destined for your server's private network (10.247.192.x) pass through ZeroTier. All your standard internet traffic (Google, YouTube, Netflix, social media) continues directly over your regular Wi-Fi or cellular connection at full speed.

⚠️ What does ACCESS_DENIED or NOT_FOUND mean?

ACCESS_DENIED: You have successfully reached the server's network, but the administrator hasn't checked the "Authorize" box yet. Send your 10-digit Node ID to the admin.
NOT_FOUND: Check the 16-digit Network ID for typing errors.

🎶 Do I need ZeroTier for music streaming or dedicated music apps?

No, ZeroTier is NOT needed for music streaming. The Aether Audio web player streams music directly in your browser. For Subsonic-compatible mobile apps (Symfonium, Ultrasonic, DSub), connect to your server's music endpoint URL without needing ZeroTier active. ZeroTier is strictly reserved for Plex video streaming.
