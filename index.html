🚀 How do I view my website after launching?

As soon as the launch finishes, a green popup appears with an Open Live Deployment link. You can also view all deployed projects at any time by clicking Hosted Sites in the top navigation bar.

🗄️ Do I need to enter passwords for the database?

No! Everything is pre-configured with standard credentials (User: root, Password: root, Port: 3307). Just type a database name and click create.

🛡️ Can I break anything by testing?

No! All websites are hosted in their own sandboxed folders under /sites/, and databases are completely isolated. It is 100% safe to experiment.

🌐 What is ZeroTier?

ZeroTier is a private, secure network tunnel. It connects your laptop or phone directly to this 2011 MacBook Pro as if you were on the same home Wi-Fi network, even when you're away.
