House Rules & Instructions 4 items
View rules Hide rules
1

Give your repository a unique name

Name your GitHub project something unique (e.g. sam-portfolio instead of just portfolio). This makes sure your site gets its own folder and won't accidentally overwrite anyone else's website.

2

Keep picture & style links simple (Relative Links)

Link your images and CSS directly from your folder (e.g. images/pic.jpg or style.css). Avoid computer file paths like C:/Users/... so your images display correctly for everyone on the internet.

3

Home server uptime & brief pauses

This server runs on a real MacBook Pro at home. If the MacBook is rebooting, updating, or taking a quick nap, websites might pause for a minute. Don't worry — they automatically come right back online!

4

100MB storage space per site

You get 100MB of SSD space for your site. That is plenty of room for web code and optimized photos! Just avoid uploading giant video clips or large zip downloads.
